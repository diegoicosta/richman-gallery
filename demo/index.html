Richman Gallery

Four galleries demonstrating the library: different column starting points (the library owns the responsive stepping below each), multi-select with a per-gallery selection bar, dynamic insertion & reinitialization, and likes & comments with their callbacks. Resize the window to see the grids step down. Click any photo to open the fullscreen viewer.

The three photo-link possibilities are shown in the viewer: photo 4 — linkTo with a title and no linkText, so the title becomes the link; photo 9 — linkTo + linkText, showing the title plus a link labeled "View photo"; photo 10 — linkTo with neither linkText nor title, falling back to "(open)".

Dynamic gallery — full insertion & reinitialization

This section starts empty. Insert a gallery appends a complete .rmg-gallery to the page — the library notices it and initializes it automatically (click-to-open, tags and likes all work immediately). Replace images swaps the grid's images and calls reinitialize() so the new images become interactive.

Reactions gallery — likes & comments

This gallery has likes and comments enabled, and sits markers in the bottom-right corner. Some photos start pre-liked (data-liked="true"). Click a heart to like/unlike, or a bubble to comment — both are reported through the onLike / onComments callbacks and logged below.

The heart is stateful: it updates immediately (optimistic), and the page can roll the change back. Tick the box to simulate a failed save and watch setLiked() restore the previous state. Comments are stateless — clicking the bubble changes nothing in the viewer; it only notifies the page.
